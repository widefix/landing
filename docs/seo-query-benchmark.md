# Search visibility benchmark

The CSV contains 23 target queries for widefix.com. Metrics are intentionally empty until a Search Console, Ahrefs, or Semrush export is available. No live rankings have been measured.

## Rails performance service positioning

The existing `/ruby-on-rails-performance` page targets **Ruby on Rails performance optimization**, with **Rails performance tuning** as a secondary phrase. Retaining the route keeps existing article and showcase links pointing to one service page instead of splitting the offer across competing URLs.

This is a terminology and service-scope decision, not a measured search-volume result. The official [Rails deployment performance guide](https://guides.rubyonrails.org/tuning_performance_for_deployment.html) uses "tuning" for server configuration and discusses latency, throughput and memory tradeoffs. "Optimization" describes our broader application-level offer, including Ruby, ActiveRecord, PostgreSQL and jobs; "improvement" describes the outcome rather than the work. Public search results did not provide a usable keyword-volume comparison during this review.

Track all three variants in the CSV and validate the choice against a Search Console or keyword-tool export before claiming one has greater demand. Keep the separate PostgreSQL consulting page focused on database-specific work. The Rails page brings together the CostaDelHome, Kajabi Experts and ShopWired case studies and relevant engineering articles without presenting their individual results as guarantees.

## Measurement guidance

For Search Console, record a fixed period and the same country, device, and search type on each run. Compare equivalent periods. Average position is an aggregate across impressions, not a fixed rank for every search. Keep brand and non-brand queries distinct and preserve queries with no reported impressions as missing data, not an invented rank.

The original “best rails maintenance companies” query and the more specific “best ruby on rails maintenance companies” variant are tracked separately to help distinguish railway-related intent.

The Worship Online case is a different property. Its two published screenshots have different visible date ranges, and the earlier image includes a filter warning. They show 26.1K and 1.02M clicks but do not establish a comparable-period 4000% uplift. Request the original exports with date selectors and filters before calculating growth. Conversion claims need their own analytics evidence.
